# Review and corrections

## Publication status

Gifted Atlas is an independently authored educational software publication. It is not a diagnostic tool, clinical guideline, systematic review, or peer-reviewed journal article. It may be cited as software using `CITATION.cff`, but a citation does not convert the site into peer-reviewed evidence.

## Evidence and review

Major claims should be traceable to the bibliography or an inline source. References are checked for title–DOI correspondence, but inclusion does not imply consensus or uniform study quality. Any future professional or academic review must state the reviewer's name, relevant credentials, date, scope, conflicts of interest, and requested changes before the site describes itself as externally reviewed.

## Corrections

Corrections should identify the affected claim, the supporting primary or high-quality synthesis source, and the reason for the change. Material changes should be recorded in a versioned GitHub release. Superseded statements should be corrected or removed rather than silently preserved as current guidance.

For durable academic citation, archive a numbered release in a preservation service such as Zenodo and cite the resulting DOI together with the version and access date.
