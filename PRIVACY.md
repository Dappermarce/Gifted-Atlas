# Privacy

Gifted Atlas is designed to minimize collection.

- The educational quizzes and search run in the browser. Answers and results are not sent to or stored by the Gifted Atlas server.
- Theme and language preferences are stored locally in the visitor's browser.
- The site does not load fonts or content images from third-party CDNs during a normal visit.
- DOI, institutional, and other external links contact their destination only after the visitor chooses them. Those destinations apply their own privacy policies.
- Routine server logs contain operational request metadata for API routes, not response bodies or quiz answers.

Clearing site data in the browser removes the stored theme and language preferences.

This policy describes the repository release dated 2026-09-23. A hosting provider may retain limited network and operational logs under its own terms.
